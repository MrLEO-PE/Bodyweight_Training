Bodyweight Builder

The PE department's home-training app. Students follow timed bodyweight workouts on their phone, log every rep, and compete with their class. Teachers set the challenges, train live with whole forms and see every result.

For students: fourteen steps, one routine

From scanning the class code to the leaderboard. The same routine works at home, in a lesson, or live with the whole form.

  1. Join the class

    Students scan the QR code on the class sheet, or type their form and class code.

  2. Pick a name, make a PIN

    They choose their name and create a 4-digit PIN. No email, no account to set up.

  3. One clear page

    This week's progress, then the teacher's challenge with its due date. Everything else waits under More.

  4. See the workout

    The exercises in order. Where the teacher allows it, each student picks the level that challenges them.

  5. Four clear stages

    Warm-up, the challenge of the day, heart rate, cool-down: a strip at the top always shows where you are.

  6. Follow the timer

    Full-screen timer with beeps and a voice coach. Exercises cannot be skipped.

  7. Log in the rest

    During each rest, students type their reps or seconds held.

  8. Last score straight away

    The last exercise has no rest: 20 seconds to write its score while it is fresh.

  9. Heart-rate check

    After the last exercise the screen turns red: find the pulse and count for 30 seconds.

  10. Write the beats

    Right after the count, 20 seconds to type the beats. Then the cool-down.

  11. Count together when live

    In a live challenge everyone waits on the red screen, and the teacher starts the 30-second count for the whole class at once.

  12. Points, bpm and effort

    The beats are doubled into bpm. Points reward reps, holds and harder levels.

  13. Live with the class

    When the teacher starts a live challenge, every student taps "I'm ready" and starts at the same second.

  14. Leaderboards and badges

    Class leaderboards, a form cup, ranks from Rookie to Legend, badges and a weekly streak.

A heart-rate check in every workout

After the last exercise and before the cool-down, the screen turns red. Students stop, find their pulse on the wrist or neck, and count for 30 seconds. They have 20 seconds to type the number, and the app doubles it into beats per minute, then the cool-down begins. In a live challenge the teacher starts the 30-second count for everyone at the same second. Teachers see every student's bpm next to their score.

Start with your classes

  1. Log in as Teacher with the department PIN, and open Teacher tutorial for the full guide.
  2. Import your class lists from Excel. The forms are created for you.
  3. Give each form its class sheet with the QR code. Students join and create their PIN in the lesson.
  4. Pick a challenge from Saved challenges and give it to your forms with a due date.
  5. Run the first live challenge in a lesson, so students learn the routine before training at home.

Safety and data

Safe by design

Every workout starts with a warm-up and ends with stretches. Each exercise has a how-to guide with a safety point. Students choose levels they can manage, and scores that look impossible are flagged for the teacher.

Data kept small

Only names, forms, gender, workouts and heart rate are stored, behind a class code and a PIN. Students see only their own data and class leaderboards. Teachers can export or back up everything, and delete old lists.
